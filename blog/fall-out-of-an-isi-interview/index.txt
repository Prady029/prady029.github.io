1a:Taaf,<h4>Not to be confused with ISI, Pakistan.</h4><blockquote>What was there to reflect upon? A personal introspection..</blockquote><figure><img alt="Indian Statistical Institute, Kolkata, India" src="https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/1024/0*vMOR6wl-sLY-7_ja.jpg" /><figcaption><strong>Indian Statistical Institute, Kolkata, India</strong></figcaption></figure><p>I had faced an interview for a research position in ISI, Kolkata recently. This is how I had prepared for that :</p><blockquote>I think its a good way to prepare for any such positions.</blockquote><ul><li>Went through the project head’s profile and went through his publications relevant to the project.</li><li>Brushed up my content that I had prepared, based on the projects that I had worked in the past.</li><li>Opened up my Deep Learning notes and had a good read.</li><li>Browsed through the machine learning and data mining slides, provided by my teachers from university.</li><li>Also looked into Bio-informatics and Soft-computing lecture scripts, that I had prepared through-out the semesters.(Well, ISI has a stronghold over this two research areas.)</li><li>Finally, some statistics and DBMS, just in case they try to test the waters.</li></ul><p>But the problem was, I did all these in <strong>last 24 hours</strong>. <em>I am lazy, I know.</em> Okay, now lets’ dive in to the interview part.</p><p>I will go question-wise in order of their appearance in my case.</p><ol><li><strong>Introduce yourself.</strong></li><li><strong>Find the output. And state your logic.</strong></li></ol><pre>int a = 2;<br>if (a &gt;&gt; 1):<br>   printf(&quot;Value of a is %d&quot;,a);</pre><p>3. <strong>What is supervised learning? How does loss function fit into it?</strong></p><p>4. <strong>How do we minimize loss?</strong></p><p>5. <strong>Does gradient descent provide convergence in general?[Yes/No]</strong></p><p>6. <strong>How do a predictive model assign labels to an unseen example?</strong></p><p>I tried to answer most of them. They recursively dug into my answers to check my depth. I believe this what they look for, at least for research positions.</p><blockquote><strong>You may get stuck at some points and its ok. I sent an answer via email just when I recalled the most appropriate answer. This was after the interview was over.</strong></blockquote><p>As an aspirant, it is you who needs to set the table straight. So, I put some questions to clarify their aims for the research problem. Being respectful is the key here.</p><blockquote>Looks simple, right! But, the key takeaway is that no matter what you prepare for, basics are the gold.
